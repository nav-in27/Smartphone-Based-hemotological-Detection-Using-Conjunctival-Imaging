# Clinical Limitations & Confounders

## 1. Ocular Pathology & Vasomotor Confounders
Conjunctival microvascular appearance reflects local tissue hemodynamics rather than systemic red blood cell mass in several circumstances:
- **Reactive Hyperemia / Vasodilation**: Allergic conjunctivitis, viral infection, ocular fatigue, dry eye syndrome, contact lens irritation, and subconjunctival hemorrhage increase mucosal erythema, potentially obscuring severe systemic anemia.
- **Vasoconstriction**: Severe cold exposure, dehydration, systemic shock, or acute stress triggers microvascular capillary bed collapse, simulating anemia pallor in healthy individuals.
- **Surface Pigmentation**: Excessive melanin deposits or pingueculae along the conjunctival margin can alter optical absorption.

## 2. Optical & Photographic Factors
- **Specular Reflection**: Tear film reflections create bright saturated glares that disrupt GLCM texture metrics.
- **ISP Tone Mapping**: Commercial smartphones apply proprietary non-linear tone curves and sharpening filters.

## 3. Screening Stance
ConjunctiAI is strictly an adjunct screening tool. It does not replace venipuncture CBC or certified point-of-care photometer tests.
